Welcome to Lembaranz.

## 1. Data Usage

This application is **Local-First**. We do not store your data on our servers. All notes, credentials, and encryption keys are stored on your device (browser IndexedDB or a local file). No account is required.

## 2. Responsibility

You are fully responsible for the security of your device and your encryption keys. We do not have a copy of the keys, so **we cannot recover your data if you forget your password and lose your recovery mnemonic**. Keep both safe.

## 3. Local Audit Ledger

Lembaranz maintains a local, hash-chained audit ledger to help you detect tampering with vault history. It stays on your device; any external anchoring of its head hash is entirely your choice and responsibility.
